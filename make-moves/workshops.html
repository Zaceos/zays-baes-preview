How It Works

Zay Comes to You

Workshops are available as one-off classes or weekend formats. Zay travels. Seattle and Tacoma are home, but if you have a studio, a community, or a city with people who need to move, pitch it.

  • Format: Single class or weekend intensive (Friday evening plus Saturday). Duration tailored to the venue.
  • Who books: Dance studios, fitness studios, community organizations, event planners, and wellness retreats.
  • Audience: Beginner to advanced. All levels can be accommodated. Specify your audience in the inquiry.
  • Tour-ready: Zay's aspiration is to bring this to cities like Miami, Chicago, Phoenix, and LA. If you are in one of those cities, pitch it first.
