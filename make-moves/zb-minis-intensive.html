Industry Training Built for Young Dancers

ZB Minis Intensive is the youth version of ZB Performance Training Co. Same industry-facing mindset, same Zay energy, redesigned from the ground up for a younger audience. No heels. Age-appropriate content. Real industry exposure.

Young dancers who are serious about the craft get the same access to industry knowledge that the adult program provides: camera work, performance coaching, Q&A with industry guests, and a foundation for understanding the business they are walking into.

What's Covered

What Young Dancers Learn

Camera Work

How to move for the camera. Where to look. How to hold the frame. Skills that transfer to auditions, reels, and social content.

Performance Training

Stage presence fundamentals. Energy placement. Performing for an audience versus dancing for yourself. The difference is real.

Industry Exposure

Q&A with industry guests. A basic introduction to the business of dance: what it looks like, what it takes, and how to start preparing now.

Brand Basics

Age-appropriate brand awareness. Introducing the idea that who you are as a performer matters alongside what you can do.

Confidence Drills

The same confidence-first methodology that defines Zay's adult programs, adapted for young dancers who need the room to take up space.

Community

A room full of young dancers with serious ambitions and the same fire. The connection matters as much as the content.

Is This Them?

The Young Dancer Who Is Ready to Take It Seriously

  • Young dancers who already have dance training and want more than a recital.
  • Kids and teens who want to understand what a career in dance actually looks like.
  • Families who want their young dancer to get real industry exposure in a safe, age-appropriate environment.

Format

Intensive workshop format. No ongoing commitment required. Age range and cohort size confirmed with each run. Dates are not currently scheduled. Sign up to be first when a new cohort opens.
