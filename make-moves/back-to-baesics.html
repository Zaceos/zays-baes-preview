What It Is

No One Starts Knowing How to Slay

Most heels programs throw you in. Back to BAEsics walks you in. Zay built this series for the woman, man, or person who has been watching from the corner, telling themselves they could not. Six weeks later, you are rolling on the floor, hair flipping, throwing the thing, and wondering why anyone ever called you shy.

Weeks one through three: no heels. You learn the movement, build the confidence, and earn the right to put the shoe on. Weeks four through six: the heels come on, and everything you built pays off in a closing combo that proves you could always do this.

Back to BAEsics has a Part 1, a Part 2, and an extended training option to further your journey. This series fills quickly. Grab a friend and jump in.

Coming soon: online cohort. Take the 6-week series from anywhere.

Includes 5 free Bila drop-in classes during the series, so you can explore salsa, bachata, and more while you train.

The Curriculum

What You Will Learn

Part I: Beginner

1No Heels

Foundation

  • Introductions and class overview
  • Stretching and ankle strengthening
  • Bevels and balance
  • Weight transitions and hip rolls
  • Swivels and whip-ups
  • Texture tips and energy placement
2No Heels

Building the Walk

  • Review Week 1 drills
  • Intro to slow walks
  • Passes
  • Confidence exercises
3No Heels

Floor Work Preview

  • Repeat Weeks 1 and 2 drills
  • Across-the-floor exercises
  • Intro to lite floor work
4Heels On

Heels Transition

  • Review Weeks 1 through 3 drills in heels
  • No floor work this week: build the feel first
5Heels On

First Combo

  • Tiny drill combo applying Weeks 1 through 4
  • No floor work: lock in the combo
6Heels On

Slay Day

  • Tiny combo review
  • Optional filming: document your transformation

Part II: Beyond Beginner

1No Heels

#BAEBOOTCAMP Begins

  • Introductions and #BAEBOOTCAMP opening
  • Bevels and balance revisited
  • Hip-sits, dips, and hairwhips
  • Texture tips: layer up
2No Heels

Sensuality and Transitions

  • #BAEBOOTCAMP warm-up
  • Review Week 1
  • Weight transitions and walks
  • Sensuality: intentionality in movement
3No Heels

Floor Work Returns

  • #BAEBOOTCAMP warm-up
  • Quick review of Weeks 1 and 2
  • Intro to lite floor work
4Heels On

Elevate

  • Review Weeks 1 through 3 in heels
  • Begin movement combination
5Heels On

The Combo

  • Tiny combo applying Weeks 1 through 4
  • Refine and embody
6Heels On

Last Day: Community and Dance

  • Final day dance: put it all together
  • Community connection: the room you built
Format

What to Expect

  • Duration: 6-week series, weekly Saturday sessions. Session length confirmed at registration.
  • Where: Bila studio, Seattle (Saturdays). Also run independently in Tacoma.
  • Included: 5 free Bila drop-in classes during the series. Explore salsa, bachata, and more while you train.
  • Level: True beginners. No prior dance experience required.
  • Who teaches: Zay, in person. Solo-taught. Every cohort.
