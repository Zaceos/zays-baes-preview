What Zay Teaches

A Method, Not Just Choreography

Zay teaches a method, not just choreography: rigorous technique built inside a room that grows real confidence, both essential, neither one first. That is what Back to BAEsics was built to be, a foundational, safe space where the fundamentals get done right before anything flashy gets added.

Level in Zay's classes is set by demonstration, not by time in a room. Beginner, intermediate, advanced: it comes down to what the dancer can show, not how many weeks they have logged. It is a distinction most classes skip, and it is the whole reason the training holds up.
