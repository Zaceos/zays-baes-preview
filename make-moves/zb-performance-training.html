The Next Level

ZB Performance Training Co. is not a beginner program. It is the program for intermediate to advanced dancers who are serious about turning their skill into a career. Industry prepped for dancers ready to work. Headshots, agent advice, performance training, and more.

The program packages technical dance training with the infrastructure you need to actually work: headshots, resume building, reel guidance, camera presence, and industry knowledge. Students leave with tools, not just technique.

ZB Performance Training Co. has not run in approximately two years while Zay focused on Back to BAEsics. A revival is actively being planned. Redesigned format: 6-day intensive over two weekends, instead of the previous multi-week format.

The Work

What You Will Walk Away With

On Your Body

In-heels and out-of-heels industry-level dance training. Performance presence coaching. Stage and camera confidence.

In Your Portfolio

Professional headshots. A resume template. Reel guidance: what to shoot, how to cut it, how to get noticed.

In Your Network

Agent advice. Industry Q&A. Pre-brand building.

Format

How It Runs

  • Duration: 6-day intensive across two weekends (approximately 2.5 to 3 hours per session).
  • Level: Intermediate to advanced. Prior dance experience required. This is not Back to BAEsics.
  • Venue: Seattle-based. Venue TBD for revival cohort.
  • Status: Program currently paused. Revival being planned for 2026. Get on the list.
Who This Is For

You Are Ready. You Just Need the Stage.

  • Dancers who want to book gigs, build a reel, or get in front of casting.
  • Back to BAEsics graduates who are ready to take the next step.
  • Trained dancers who have the skill but need the infrastructure to monetize it.
  • Anyone who wants to dance on camera, on stage, or on payroll.
