What Fits

The Brands That Belong in Zay's Room

Dance and Fitness

Activewear, dancewear, footwear, and fitness brands. The audience lives in studios and on stages. They notice what Zay wears.

Heels and Footwear

Heels brands that want to reach the community that actually trains in their product. The Back to BAEsics audience is the exact target.

Beauty and Lifestyle

Beauty, wellness, and lifestyle brands with a strong female-empowerment or creative point of view. The energy matches.

Fashion and Apparel

Fashion brands that want the dance and creative community. Especially brands with a bold, confident aesthetic.

Female Empowerment

Any brand whose core positioning is confidence, empowerment, and helping people take up space. That is Zay's entire brand language.

Creative and Cultural

Brands with a strong creative point of view that want reach into Seattle's dance and arts community and beyond.

How It Can Work

Engagement Formats

Ambassador Roles

Ongoing brand representation. Zay wears, uses, and advocates for the product with the credibility of someone who actually uses it.

Content Collaborations

Co-created content across Zay's platforms. Social, video, in-class, or event-based. Zay's audience meets your brand organically.

Branded Class Series

A Back to BAEsics or ZB Classes series sponsored by the brand. Co-branded, in-studio, with real participants and real content capture.

Product Partnerships

Product featured in class. Gifted to students. Used in performance. Zay curates what goes in the room; if it belongs, it shows.

Event Sponsorships

Sponsored presence at Brides BAEs, corporate events, ZB Performance Training cohort showcases, and workshops.

Something Else

Have a format in mind that isn't on this list? Pitch it. Zay is open to creative arrangements that make sense for the audience.

The Audience

Who Zay Reaches

Zay's audience is built across Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube, plus an in-person student base from Back to BAEsics, ZB Classes, and event clients. The community is engaged, and the brand has trust where it counts. Students do not just take his class. They invite their friends, buy the products he recommends, and show up to his events.
