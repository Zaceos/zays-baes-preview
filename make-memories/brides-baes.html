How It Works

From Inquiry to Your Best Day

  1. 1

    Consultation

    Zay learns your vision: the theme, the vibe, the crowd, the date, and what you actually want the experience to feel like.

  2. 2

    Playlist and Theme Planning

    Music locked in. Theme and props confirmed. Choreography concept approved.

  3. 3

    Choreography Session(s)

    Depending on the format: one or two sessions to learn the material. Zay keeps it accessible for guests who have never danced in their lives.

  4. 4

    The Day

    You show up. You dance. Everyone loses it. That's the plan.
